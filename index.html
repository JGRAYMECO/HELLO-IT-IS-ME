Big Tech, power and
democratic governance.

Joanne E. Gray is an Associate Professor of Media and Communications at the University of Sydney and Editor-in-Chief of Policy & Internet. Her research examines how global technology companies acquire and exercise power, and what this means for democratic governance.

Working across political economy, law and public policy, she studies Big Tech, digital sovereignty, AI infrastructure, platform regulation and emerging technologies. Her research has informed Australian technology policy and regulation, including work on online safety, privacy and platform accountability.
