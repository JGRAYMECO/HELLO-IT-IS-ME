Recent examples

Social media and young people

A 2024 submission I co-authored to the Joint Select Committee on Social Media and Australian Society was cited 17 times in the committee’s final report.

#FixOurFeeds

In 2025 the advocacy organisation Teach Us Consent sought my expertise on regulating social media algorithms. I advised that reform should go beyond the European Union’s opt-out model and require a default opt-in, a position that shaped the organisation’s #FixOurFeeds campaign. In September 2026 the Australian Government announced that its Digital Duty of Care legislation will require platforms to give users tools to opt out of algorithmic feeds.

Smart glasses and immersive technologies

Through the ARC Discovery Project Governing Immersive Tech, which I lead, our research has reached ministers, regulators and advocates.

  • Briefings to the Attorney-General, the Hon Michelle Rowland MP, Assistant Minister Ged Kearney MP and several New South Wales parliamentarians on privacy, affirmative consent and the gender-based harms of AI-enabled wearables and smart glasses. The Attorney-General later referred to our research in Question Time.
  • Briefings to Australia’s four digital platform regulators (the ACCC, ACMA, the eSafety Commissioner and the Office of the Australian Information Commissioner) on emerging harms from immersive technologies.
  • Governing Social Virtual Reality was cited six times in the Digital Platform Regulators Forum’s 2025 working paper on immersive technologies, and our research is cited in the eSafety Commissioner’s 2025 position statement on immersive technologies.
